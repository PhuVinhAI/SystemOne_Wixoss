## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What is 【Soul】?**
A. LRIG may be attached to SIGNI as 【Soul】 by effect. You cannot attach more than two 【Soul】 on a SIGNI. If the SIGNI with a 【Soul】 attached to it leaves the field, 【Soul】 will be put into its owner's LRIG trash by Rule-based action.

**Q. When my SIGNI attacks with this card attached to it as a 【Soul】, I vanished target SIGNI in front of it by its [Auto] ability. Can I deal damage to my opponent by my attacked SIGNI?**
A. After [Auto] ability triggered when your SIGNI attacking, if your opponent's SIGNI in front of my SIGNI who attacked leaves from the field, when your attacked SIGNI's battle has not occurred and it starts processing your attacked SIGNI, it can deal damage to your opponent because there is no SIGNI in front of it.
