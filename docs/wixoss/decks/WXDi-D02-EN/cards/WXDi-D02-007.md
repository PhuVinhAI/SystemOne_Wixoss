## FAQ chính thức

**Q. There is only one SIGNI of level 2 or lower in opponent's field.
How will this [Enter] be processed?**
A. In that case, the two targets chosen by this effect will both choose that SIGNI. Since the effects are processed in order from top to bottom, the SIGNI will be placed in the Trash (not returned to your opponent's hand).
