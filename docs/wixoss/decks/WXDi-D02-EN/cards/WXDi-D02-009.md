## FAQ chính thức

**Q. When is the right time to select a target?**
A. As a general rule, the target is chosen just before the ability is activated, but if the text of the ability says "then", it will process to the front of "then" and then take the target again.
So, put three cards from the top of the deck on Trash, and then target the two SIGNI cards on Trash.
