## Card text chính thức

[Action] {1/Turn} Discard a card: This LRIG gains "[Auto] {1/Turn}: When this LRIG attacks, choose one of the following. ①Draw a card. ②【Ener Charge 1】. ③Put target SIGNI on your opponent's field into its owner's trash." until end of turn.
[Action] {1/Game} {Redx0}: Make up to two target SIGNI on your opponent's field 《Servant ZERO》 until end of turn.
(《Servant ZERO》 are colorless level one <Primal Spirit> SIGNI with power 1000 and no abilities.)

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. Do effects that modify the power, increase or decrease the power, or grant abilities to the original SIGNI remain active when my opponent's SIGNI is made into 《Servant ZERO》?**
A. If a SIGNI on the field becomes another SIGNI, effects that modify its power, increasing or decreasing its power, or granting it abilities are not applied.

**Q. If a SIGNI with an [Auto] ability that triggers on "leave the field" becomes 《Servant ZERO》 due to this LRIG's effect, will the [Auto] ability of "leave the field" still trigger if that SIGNI is banished?**
A. No, it will not trigger. That SIGNI has left the field as 《Servant ZERO》.
