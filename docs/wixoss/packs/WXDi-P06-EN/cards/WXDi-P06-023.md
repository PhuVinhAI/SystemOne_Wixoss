## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What happens if the second [Enter] ability returns this card to the LRIG deck?**
A. The level one LRIG that was under this card becomes the top one. The level one LRIG's [Enter] ability is not triggered because that LRIG was underneath is not entered.

**Q. At the end of turn when the second [Enter] ability triggers, my opponent pays {Colorless} and the card remains on the field. What happens at the end of the next turn?**
A. The second [Enter] ability is triggered only at the end of the turn when it was enter the field at that turn. Nothing happens at the end of the next turn.

**Q. What happens if the second [Enter] ability returns this card to my LRIG deck and the limit is reduced so that the total level of SIGNI on the field exceeds the limit?**
A. In such a case, put a SIGNI into your trash so that it fits within the limit by Rule-based action when that card returns to your LRIG deck.
