## Card text chính thức

[Auto]: Whenever this SIGNI attacks, until end of turn, all SIGNI on your opponent's field gets -1000 power for each <Virtual> SIGNI on your field.
[Enter]: If 《Fuyuki Hakase, Code 2434》 is on your field, target SIGNI on your opponent's field gets -4000 power until end of turn.

## Power text / Life Burst

Add up to two target <Virtual> SIGNI from your trash to your hand.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. If the number of <Virtual> SIGNIs in your field changes after applying a power minus effect to an opponent's SIGNI with [Auto].**
A. Even if the number of <Virtual>SIGNI increases or decreases after [Auto] is activated, the value of the power minus effect will not change until the end of the turn.
