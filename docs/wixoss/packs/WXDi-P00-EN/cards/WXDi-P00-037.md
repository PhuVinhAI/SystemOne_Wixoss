## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What does it mean by [Team]?**
A. It means the team indicated by that card. The [Team Auto], [Team Action], [Team Constant], and [Team Enter] abilities of the card are valid only if all 3 LRIGs in your field are the indicated [Team].
In the case of this card, if all three of your LRIGs are <Sanbaka>, you will be able to use the [Team Action] ability.

**Q. If there is only one of <Virtual> SIGNI  in my trash when I activate [Enter], can I add it to my hand?**
A. Yes, you can. Since the effect targets "up to two" targets, you can activate it on only one target.
