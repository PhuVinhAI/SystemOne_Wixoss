## FAQ chính thức

**Q. Can I put 《Servant #》 in my opponent's Ener zone on Trash?**
A. Yes, you can. 《Servant #》 is a colorless card, as indicated by the icon in the upper right corner. Since colorless cards are treated as ”having no color”, they meet the requirements of this ability regardless of the color of Center LRIG.

**Q. When I pay cost for [Enter], I have only two red cards in my hand. Can I discard those two cards and get [Assassin]?**
A. No, you can't. If you don't have enough cards in your hand to discard as cost, you can't discard any cards and you can't activate the effect.

**Q. Can I discard 《Servant #》as a red card for [Enter]'s cost?**
A. No, you can't. [Multi Ener] is an ability that only works when you paying Ener costs. So, you can't discard it as a red card.
