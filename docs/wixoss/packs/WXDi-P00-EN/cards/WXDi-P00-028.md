## FAQ chính thức

**Q. Can the LRIG that was upped by this effect attack again?**
A. Yes, it can.

**Q. Which comes first, the LRIG being upped by this ability or the LRIG's attack dealing damage?**
A. Up first: when you attack with LRIG, this ability will activate and up your LRIG, then it will check the opponent's [Guard], and if it is not [Guard], it will damage the opponent. After that, if the LRIG is still up, you can attack again.
