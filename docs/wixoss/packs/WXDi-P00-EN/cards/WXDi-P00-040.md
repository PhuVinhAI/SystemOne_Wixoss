## FAQ chính thức

**Q. If a SIGNI with [Enter] is put into play by this effect, does it trigger?**
A. Yes, it does.

**Q. Is it possible to not use the [Enter] ability?**
A. No, [Enter] ability with no cost (described before":") is compulsory. In this case, If there is an appropriate target, it should always be selected as the target. However, if there are already three SIGNIs in your SIGNI ZONE, or if the targeted SIGNI exceeds the LEVEL or LIMIT of your LRIG, it will not be put onto your SIGNI ZONE.
