## Card text chính thức

[Enter]: Put the top two cards of your deck into your trash. Then, add up to one target red SIGNI and one target black SIGNI from your trash to your hand.

[Auto]: When this card is put into the LRIG Trash as an Exceed cost, you may discard a card. If you do, add target red or black SIGNI from your trash to your hand.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. [Auto] ability triggered when put this card into its owner's trash as Exceed's cost, and some ability played by Exceed's cost. Which of [Auto] ability and some ability will be resolved first?**
A. If you paid A as a cost and declare play some ability will be processed first. Then, you process the triggered [Auto] ability of this card.

**Q. When I put the top two cards of my deck into my trash by this card's [Enter] ability, may I add those two cards with red and black from my trash to my hand?**
A. Yes, you can. You target up two cards after you put two cards into your trash from your deck.

**Q. If my trash has only red or black SIGNIs, may I add target SIGNI from my trash to my hand by this card's [Enter] ability?**
A. Yes, you may add only one card from your trash to your hand, even if SIGNIs in your trash are only one of either color.
