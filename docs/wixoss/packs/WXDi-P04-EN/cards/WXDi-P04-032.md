## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What is the specific effect of the first [Auto] ability?**
A. Triggers when an opponent's ability or effect targets this SIGNI. For example, if this SIGNI is targeted by an effect such as "vanish target SIGNI.", this first [Enter] ability will trigger. This triggered [Auto] ability resolves after your opponent's effect or ability has been processed. This example resolves this [Auto] ability after being vanished and choose one of your opponent's SIGNI Zones.

**Q. I put a SIGNI with [Enter] ability to target SIGNI onto my field. My opponent has only "Exia, Blessed Angel Queen" on your opponent's field. May I choose not to target "Exia, Blessed Angel Queen" with my SIGNI's [Enter] ability to target SIGNI?**
A. No, you cannot. [Enter] ability with no cost must triggers. And if there is a SIGNI that can be targeted, the target must be chosen. However, in the case of "target up to one SIGNI" or "target up to two SIGNIs", you may choose to not target.
