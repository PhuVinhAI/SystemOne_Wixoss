## Card text chính thức

[Constant]: As your opponent places a SIGNI on their field, they must place it in front of this SIGNI if possible.
[Constant]: During your turn, this SIGNI cannot be moved from the field to anywhere other than the Ener Zone by your opponent's effects.
[Auto]: Whenever a SIGNI on your opponent's field is placed in front of this SIGNI, you may give that SIGNI -3000 power until end of turn.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. If there is already a my opponent's SIGNI on the SIGNI Zone in front of this SIGNI, can my opponent place a SIGNI on another SIGNI Zone?**
A. Yes, if there is already a SIGNI, or if the placement cannot be made on that SIGNI Zone, it can be placed on another SIGNI Zone.

**Q. If I put two of these SIGNIs on the field, how can my opponent place his/her SIGNI?**
A. If both SIGNI Zones in front of these two SIGNIs are placeable, your opponent can put SIGNI into either of them. If one of them is occupied, the SIGNI can be placed in front of the other.

**Q. What exactly is the second [Constant] ability?**
A. It mainly prevents effects such as returning this card to your hand, putting it into your Trash, or removing it from the game, and allows it to stay on the field.
Since it prevents the card from moving to zones other than the Ener Zone, it cannot prevent effects that put this card into your Ener Zoneand effects that vanish the card.
