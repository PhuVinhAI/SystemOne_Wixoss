## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What exactly is the effect of [Auto] ability?**
A. It is triggered when this SIGNI is targeted by your opponent's ability or effect.
 For example, if this SIGNI is targeted by your opponent's SIGNI's [Enter] ability, it will trigger after that opponent's SIGNI's ability is processed.
 Even if it is a vanishing effect, the ability is still triggered, so it will process after this SIGNI is vanished, then you can draw a card.
