## FAQ chính thức

**Q. Are cards moved by [Ener Charge 1] performed with [Auto] ability also counted as cards moved from the deck to the Ener Zone in the subsequent conditions?**
A. Yes, they are counted. For example, if you performed [Ener Charge 2] in that turn, a total of 3 or more cards will be moved by this SIGNI attacking, so the condition is met.

**Q. By this SIGNI's [Auto] ability, when I attacked, I vanished my opponent's SIGNI in front of this SIGNI. Does this attack deal damage to my opponent?**
A. If your opponent's SIGNI in front of you leaves the field after the [Auto] ability triggered when it attacks, it will deal damage to the opponent because there is no SIGNI in front at the time the attack itself is processed.
