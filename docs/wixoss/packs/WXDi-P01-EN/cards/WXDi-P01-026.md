## Card text chính thức

[Enter]: Each player shuffles all cards in their SIGNI Zone and trash back into their deck, then looks at the top seven cards of their deck. Each player may put any number of SIGNI from among their seven cards onto their field, and puts the rest into the trash. The [Enter] abilities of SIGNI put onto the field this way do not activate.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. The correct procedure for this [Enter].**
A. The correct procedure is as follows.
1.Each player shuffles their SIGNI zone and the cards in trash, adding them to the deck.
2.Each player looks at 7 cards from the top of his or her deck.
3.The turn player chooses the number of SIGNI he or she wants to put on the field. （At this time, only the number of cards is shown face down.)
4.The non-turn player chooses any number of SIGNI to put on the field.  (Same as above.)
5.The turn player decides which SIGNI zone to put each SIGNI on the field face up.
6.The non-turn player decides which SIGNI zone to put each SIGNI to be put on the field, face up.(At this time, the non-turn player can check the turn player's placement before deciding where to put it out.)
7.Each SIGNI goes into the field at the same time. At this time, the unselected cards are put into the trash at the same time too.
