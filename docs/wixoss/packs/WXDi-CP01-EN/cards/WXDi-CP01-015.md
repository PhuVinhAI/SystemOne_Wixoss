## Card text chính thức

[Enter]: Put target level two or more SIGNI on your opponent's field into its owner's trash. Your opponent reveals the top card of their deck until they turn over a SIGNI with a level less than it. They put that SIGNI onto their field. The [Enter] abilities of SIGNI put onto their field this way do not activate. They put cards revealed this way on the bottom of their deck in a random order.
[Enter] {White} {Colorless}: Add target SIGNI with a {Guard Icon} from your trash to your hand.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. I put my opponent's level two SIGNI into its owner's trash by the first [Enter] ability. Then my opponent continued to reveal their deck until they turned over SIGNI of lower level than it, but there was no corresponding SIGNI and the deck was reduced to zero cards. What happens?**
A. If your opponent reveals the top of card of thier deck and reduced to zero without revealed lower level than the SIGNI put into thier trash, your opponent cannot put SIGNI on thier field, the revealed cards are shuffled back into the deck and the effect ends.

**Q. Can I add the card paid for as the cost of the [Enter] ability directly into my hand by this effect of [Enter] ability?**
A. Yes, it is possible. After paying the cost of the [Enter] ability, target a card that meets the conditions of your trash.

**Q. Does the [Enter] ability have to be triggered?**
A. The [Enter] ability without cost must be triggered, so the first [Enter] ability must be triggered. If your opponent has a level two or higher SIGNI, you target it and deal with the effect of the first [Enter] ability.
Like the second [Enter] ability, you can choose to trigger an [Enter] ability with cost by paying cost or not  to trigger by not paying cost.
