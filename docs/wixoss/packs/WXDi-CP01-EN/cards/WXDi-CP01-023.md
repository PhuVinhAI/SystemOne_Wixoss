## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What happens if two Life Cloth are crushed by my opponent's SIGNI with [Double Crush]?**
A. For each crush, you can choose to put five cards from the top of your deck into your trash. If you have ten or more cards in your deck, you can choose to put ten cards into your trash and none of them will be crushed.

**Q. When two Life Cloth are crushed by my opponent's SIGNI with [Double Crush], can I replace the first crush and put five cards from the top of my deck into my trash and look at these cards before deciding whether to replace the second crush?**
A. No, you cannot. [Double Crush] crushes two Life Cloth at the same time, so you must decide whether to replace each crush with this effect before processing it.
