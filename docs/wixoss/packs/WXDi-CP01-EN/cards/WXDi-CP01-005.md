## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What is the effect of "Invite two Collab Livers."?**
A. Once you activate this effect, you get two "Liver token". It can be used in the effect "Collaborate with a Collab Liver". The tokens used are removed from the game.

**Q. What is 【Shadow - SIGNI】?**
A. SIGNI with 【Shadow - SIGNI】 is not targeted by the abilities or effects of SIGNI. It is likewise not targeted by the Life Burst of SIGNI.

**Q. What happens if there is a downed 《Remember//Memoria, Code: Heart》 on my opponent's field when "As you [Guard], you may pay {colorless} and collaborate with a Collab Liver" is performed by the [Const] ability?**
A. In this case, you must pay an additional {colorless} due to the [Const] ability of your opponent's 《Remember//Memoria, Code: Heart》.

**Q. Is the [Auto] ability of 《Lemon Tetra, Phantom Aquatic Beast》 triggered if I used the ability of "As you [Guard], you may pay {colorless} and collaborate with a Collab Liver"?**
A. Yes, it is triggered. Since [Guard] has been used, "[Auto]: Whenever you 【Guard】, draw a card." of 《Lemon Tetra, Phantom Aquatic Beast》 is triggered.
