## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What is the effect of "Invite two Collab Livers."?**
A. Once you activate this effect, you get two "Liver token". It can be used in the effect "Collaborate with a Collab Liver". The tokens used are removed from the game.

**Q. What happens if this LRIG attacks after gaining the [Auto] ability by the second [Action] ability?**
A. This LRIG attacks and its [Auto] ability is triggered. Your opponent pays {colorless} {colorless} {colorless} {colorless} or discards a card with [Guard], or chooses to do neither. If your opponent chooses to do neither, damage is dealt to your opponent and crush a Life Cloth if they have Life Cloth. If the crushed Life Cloth has Life Burst, it is triggered. Then your opponent chooses to [Guard] or not, and if not, damage is dealt by this LRIG's attack.
