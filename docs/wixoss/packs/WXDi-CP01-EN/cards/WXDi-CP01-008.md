## FAQ chính thức

**Q. What is the effect of "Invite two Collab Livers."?**
A. Once you activate this effect, you get two "Liver token". It can be used in the effect "Collaborate with a Collab Liver". The tokens used are removed from the game.

**Q. What happens if either player has ten or less cards in their deck when the second [Action] ability is used?**
A. In that case, the player's deck is temporarily reduced to zero cards, but no refresh is performed during the processing of the effect of this ability, so process this [Action] ability to the end first. Even if your deck is zero cards, no refresh is performed if you returned three cards into your deck from your trash during the latter part of the effect.
