## Card text chính thức

Draw two cards and put up to one 《Hameln, Master Trickster》 from your hand onto your field. Then, target 《Hameln, Master Trickster》 on your field gains "[Constant]: This SIGNI's attack is not negated by this SIGNI's effect" until end of turn.

## Power text / Life Burst

You may discard a card. If you do, put target upped SIGNI on your opponent's field on the bottom of its owner's deck.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. My opponent gave 《Hameln, Master Trickster》 who gained the [Constant] ability by playing this card the "Cannot Attack" ability. May 《Hameln, Master Trickster》 attack?**
A. No, it cannot attack. The "attack is not negated" that 《Hameln, Master Trickster》 gains with this card only ignores the "attack is negated" effect, not the "cannot attack" effect in the first place.
