## FAQ chính thức

**Q. After I put this LRIG onto my field and resolve its first [Enter] ability, may my opponent put a new SIGNI with power 10000 or less onto my opponent's field and attack?**
A. No, your opponent cannot attack. The first [Enter] ability means that SIGNI with power 10000 or less cannot attack this turn, including SIGNI that enter the field later.

**Q. May I discard three cards from my hand with the second [Enter] ability and target SIGNI get -15000 power?**
A. No, you cannot.

**Q. This card triggered the second [Enter] ability when only one SIGNI on my opponent's field. If I discard two cards from my hand, may I target that SIGNI and get -5000 power?**
A. No, you cannot target it. Even if one SIGNI on your opponent's field, you may discard two or three cards from your hand, but you must target two or three SIGNIs. if you cannot target two SIGNIs, you cannot target that SIGNI, and the effect ends without anything happening.
