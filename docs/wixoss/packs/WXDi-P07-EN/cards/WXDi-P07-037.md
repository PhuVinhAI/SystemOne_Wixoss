## FAQ chính thức

**Q. Your opponent's infected SIGNI gained "[Constant]: his SIGNI cannot attack" by this [Enter] ability. If the SIGNI is no longer Infected (e.g. by moving to the SIGNI Zone), may it attack?**
A. No, it cannot. If your opponent's SIGNI gained the ability until end of turn, remain the ability until end of turn, even if the SIGNI is no longer infected. Whether the SIGNI is infected or not is checked only when the [Enter] ability is triggered.

**Q. What is 【Virus】?**
A. A card effect may put 【Virus】 on the SIGNI Zone. You can put up to one 【Virus】 per SIGNI Zone.
The SIGNI in the same Signi Zone as 【Virus】 become infected. Nothing happens just by being infected, but the effect may refer to the infected state.
