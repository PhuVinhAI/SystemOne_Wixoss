## Card text chính thức

[Constant]: If the SIGNI in front of this SIGNI is vanished during an attack phase, it is put into the trash instead of the Ener Zone.
[Auto]: At the beginning of your attack phase, declare a number. Reveal the top card of your opponent's deck. If that card is a SIGNI with the same level as the declared number, draw a card.
[Action] {Coin Icon}: Your opponent reveals their hand and the top card of their deck.

## Power text / Life Burst

Put target upped SIGNI on your opponent's field into its owner's trash.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. During the Attack Phase, if the SIGNI in front of this SIGNI has the "cannot be vanished" ability, may this SIGNI vanish it and put it into my opponent's trash?**
A. No, you cannot. If it is a SIGNI that cannot be Vanished in the first place, you cannot even try to replace it with this [Constant] ability, and the SIGNI remains on the field.

**Q. Do cards revealed by this [Action] ability remain revealed forever?**
A. No. After the [Action] ability has been used and the player controlling the 《Yukime//Memoria, Blessed Warlord》 has confirmed revealed cards, them return to Hidden zone.
