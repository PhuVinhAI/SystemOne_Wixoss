## FAQ chính thức

**Q. Does the [Enter] ability must trigger?**
A. Yes, the [Enter] ability without cost is must triggered. You must trigger the effect of drawing three cards, but you may choose not to put any SIGNI from your hand. If you put a SIGNI, you must vanish a SIGNI on your opponent's field that meets the condition, if there is one.

**Q. When my center LRIG is 《Hirana, a Step Towards the Glimmer》 and I put a SIGNI into the center SIGNI zone by the effect of 《Ril, in Prayer》, do I include the +3000 power from the [Const] ability of 《Hirana, a Step Towards the Glimmer》 when vanishing my opponent's SIGNI with lower power than that SIGNI?**
A. Yes, you should calculate the +3000 power from the [Const] ability of 《Hirana, a Step Towards the Glimmer》 when vanishing your opponent's SIGNI with lower power than the SIGNI placed in the center SIGNI zone by the effect of 《Ril, in Prayer》. The [Const] ability takes effect immediately when the condition is met, so the placed SIGNI already has +3000 power on the field.
