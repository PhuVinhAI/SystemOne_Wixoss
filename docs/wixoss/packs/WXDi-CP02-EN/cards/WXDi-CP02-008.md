## FAQ chính thức

**Q. How can I use the [Relationship Action] ability?**
A. The effect of cards are to “Start a relationship with the student” and if you declare student's name from Blue Archive at that time, the [Releationship Action] abilitiy will be enabled. The relationship will never be lost, so once it is enabled, it can be used every turn thereafter.

**Q. If I discard my opponent's card in their hand by the second ability, does it count as the number of “discarded cards from their hand” for the [Auto] ability?**
A. No, it does not count the number of your opponents cards that were discarded. The condition of the [Auto] ability counts the number of cards you discarded from your hand.
