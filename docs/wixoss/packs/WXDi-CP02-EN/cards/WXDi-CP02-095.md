## Card text chính thức

[Enter] Put a <Blue Archive> card from your Ener Zone into your trash: You may move target SIGNI on your opponent's field to a different SIGNI Zone. It gets -3000 power until end of turn.

[Relationship Auto]: At the beginning of your attack phase, target SIGNI on your opponent's field gets -2000 power until end of turn.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. If I place my opponent's SIGNI into another SIGNI Zone by the [Enter] ability, if there is already a SIGNI, does it get swapped?**
A. No, this effect is not a swapping ability, so it cannot move to the SIGNI Zone where there is already a SIGNI.

**Q. How can I use the [Relationship Auto] ability?**
A. The effect of cards are to “Start a relationship with the student” and if you declare student's name from Blue Archive at that time, the [Releationship Auto] abilitiy will be enabled. The relationship will never be lost.
