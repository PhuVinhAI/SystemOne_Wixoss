## Card text chính thức

[Enter]: This LRIG gains "[Constant]: SIGNI on your field get +5000 power" until the end of your opponent's next end phase.
[Enter]: Add target SIGNI with a {Guard Icon} from your trash to your hand.

[Relationship Enter]: Look at the top seven cards of your deck. Reveal a <Blue Archive> card from among them and add it to your hand. Put the rest on the bottom of your deck in a random order.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. Does the [Const] ability gained by the first [Enter] ability also give +5000 power to the SIGNI that enters the field later?**
A. Yes, until the end of your next opponent's turn, any SIGNI that enters your field later will also get +5000 power.

**Q. How can I use the [Relationship Enter] ability?**
A. The effect of cards are to “Start a relationship with the student” and if you declare student's name from Blue Archive at that time, the [Releationship Enter] abilitiy will be enabled. [Relationship Enter] ability is be trigger when enter the field after started a relationship student.
