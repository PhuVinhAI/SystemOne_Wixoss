## FAQ chính thức

**Q. How can I use the [Relationship Action] ability?**
A. The effect of cards are to “Start a relationship with the student” and if you declare student's name from Blue Archive at that time, the [Releationship Action] abilitiy will be enabled. The relationship will never be lost, so once it is enabled, it can be used every turn thereafter.

**Q. What kind of ability does [S Lancer] have?**
A. When a SIGNI with [S Lancer] vanishes an opponent's SIGNI by battle, it crushes an opponent's Life Cloth, and if the opponent has no Life Cloth, it deals damage to the opponent. Even if a SIGNI has more than one [Lancer] or [S Lancer], it can only be triggered once in one battle.
