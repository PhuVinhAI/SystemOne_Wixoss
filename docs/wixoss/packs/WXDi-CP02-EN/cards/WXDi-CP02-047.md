## FAQ chính thức

**Q. If my opponent's deck is reduced to zero cards in the process of the first [Enter] ability, does it refresh?**
A. If the deck is reduced to zero cards, it is refreshed by the Rule-based action processing, but the rule processing is not performed during the processing of the effect, so this first [Enter] ability (repeated three times) is processed fully first. At that time, if the deck is zero cards, the effect of putting cards from the top of the deck into the trash is skipped. After processing the first [Enter] ability, refresh is performed.

**Q. How can I use the [Relationship Enter] ability?**
A. The effect of cards are to “Start a relationship with the student” and if you declare student's name from Blue Archive at that time, the [Releationship Enter] abilitiy will be enabled. [Relationship Enter] ability is be trigger when enter the field after started a relationship student.
