[Constant]: During your opponent's turn, other <Blue Archive> SIGNI on your field get +2000 power.
[Auto]: Whenever this SIGNI attacks, you may put three <Blue Archive> cards from your Ener Zone into your trash. If you do, up it and it loses its abilities until end of turn.

[Relationship Auto]: At the beginning of your attack phase, this SIGNI gets +5000 power until the end of your opponent's next end phase.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. After getting +5000 power by the [Relationship Auto] ability, this SIGNI lost the abilities by the [Auto] ability. Does it also lose the +5000 power gained by the [Auto] ability?**
A. No, losing abilities only erases the text, and does not affect the values outside of the text, such as power and level, so the power will remain +5000 power. However, it also loses its [Const] ability, so the effect of "other <Blue Archive> SIGNI on your field get +2000 power" will be lost.

**Q. How can I use the [Relationship Auto] ability?**
A. The effect of cards are to “Start a relationship with the student” and if you declare student's name from Blue Archive at that time, the [Releationship Auto] abilitiy will be enabled. The relationship will never be lost.
