## FAQ chính thức

**Q. May I use the second [Action] ability when my deck has six or less cards?**
A. Yes, you can. In that case, it looks at your whole decks of six cards or less, adds up to two cards to your hand, and shuffles the rest to your deck. After this effect, if your deck is reduced to zero cards, refresh it, and if there is more than one card left in your deck, the game proceeds as is.

**Q. How can I use the [Relationship Action] ability?**
A. The effect of cards are to “Start a relationship with the student” and if you declare student's name from Blue Archive at that time, the [Releationship Action] abilitiy will be enabled. The relationship will never be lost, so once it is enabled, it can be used every turn thereafter.
