## FAQ chính thức

**Q. How can I use the [Relationship Action] ability?**
A. The effect of cards are to “Start a relationship with the student” and if you declare student's name from Blue Archive at that time, the [Releationship Action] abilitiy will be enabled. The relationship will never be lost, so once it is enabled, it can be used every turn thereafter.

**Q. May I put only one target card in my trash onto the field by the first [Action] ability?**
A. No, this ability must target two cards, so only if only one can be targeted, it cannot be put onto the field.

**Q. After the [Auto] ability resolves, does SIGNI that enter my opponent's field later in the same turn also get -3000 power?**
A. No, only the SIGNI on your opponent's field when this effect triggers get -3000 power until end of turn.

**Q. May I use the first [Action] ability when only one space is available in my SIGNI Zone?**
A. Yes, you can, if you can target two appropriate SIGNI from your trash. In that case, one of the two targeted cards is put onto the field.

**Q. May I put SIGNI that I paid for as {colorless} Ener onto the field by the [Relationship Action] ability?**
A. Yes, you can put it. Pay {nothing} {nothing} as a cost, then target SIGNI in your trash.
