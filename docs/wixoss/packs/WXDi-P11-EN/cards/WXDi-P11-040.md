## Card text chính thức

[Constant]: During your opponent's turn, as your opponent chooses targets for an ability or effect, they must target this SIGNI if possible.
[Auto] {1/Turn}: During your opponent's turn, when this SIGNI on your field becomes the target of your opponent's ability or effect, another target SIGNI on your field gains 【Shadow】 until end of turn.

## Power text / Life Burst

Draw a card. Then, you may add one of your Life Cloth to your hand. If you do, add a card from your hand to your Life Cloth.

## FAQ chính thức

**Q. When this SIGNI is on my field, what happens if my opponent chooses to target it with the "target two SIGNI" ability?**
A. One of the two must always target this SIGNI and the other targets another SIGNI.

**Q. When this SIGNI is on my field, my opponent paid an Exceed cost to use 《Darkness Seven》. my opponet will choose SIGNI that gets -2000 power and SIGNI that gets -7000 power, do both need to target this SIGNI?**
A. Yes, 《Darkness Seven》 can target the same SIGNI for twice. Both can target that SIGNI, so both target that SIGNI.

**Q. When adding one of Life Cloth to my hand by Life Burst, may I add any Life Cloth I want to my hand?**
A. No. When you move Life Cloth, you need to move it from the top first. The topmost Life Cloth is added to your hand and one card from your hand is added to the top of your Life Cloth.
